F-10 Construction & Renovation

F-10 Is a Settled Family Sector — Most Work Is Improvement, Not Replacement

F-10 sits beside Fatima Jinnah Park and around its own Markaz, and it has the feel of a settled family sector rather than a rebuild market. The housing stock is younger than F-6 or F-7, plots are mostly 10 Marla and 1 Kanal, and a large share of the houses here are still the original builds — sound, but increasingly dated in layout, services and finishes.

That produces steady demand for renovation, kitchen and bathroom remodelling, service replacement and second-storey additions rather than wholesale rebuilds. Al Sadat Builders' work in F-10 is mostly this kind of project: keeping a structurally sound house, and bringing it up to a standard its owners are happy to live in for another twenty years.

Key point: Before quoting a renovation we check the existing wiring, plumbing and waterproofing. Replacing those while the walls are already open costs a fraction of doing it later after the new finishes are in.

Local Conditions

What Matters on an F-10 Project

Most F-10 work happens inside and around a house that stays standing.

🔌

Replacing Services While Walls Are Open

Original wiring and plumbing in a 20 to 30 year old house rarely meets current demand. We replace them during the renovation rather than patching, because doing it later means reopening finished walls.

⬆️

Second-Storey Additions on Existing Footings

Many F-10 houses were built for a single storey. Before adding above, we verify the existing footings and design strengthening where required so the addition does not overload what is underneath.

💧

Damp, Efflorescence & Plinth Protection

Older houses commonly show rising damp and salt staining. We address the cause — DPC condition, plinth protection, drainage falls — instead of repeatedly repainting over the symptom.

🏠

Living Through a Renovation

Many F-10 clients stay in the house during the work. We phase the project and sequence dusty operations so parts of the home remain usable throughout.

Approvals & Compliance

CDA Approvals in F-10

Extensions count as construction work here — they need approval too.

  • Building plan approval from CDA is required for extensions and structural changes, not only for new builds.
  • Adding a storey changes the approved height and covered area, so the addition must be submitted and approved before work starts.
  • Setback lines and covered-area limits apply to your specific plot — we verify these before designing any extension.
  • Structural design must satisfy Islamabad's seismic zone requirements and is subject to vetting.
  • Completion certificates follow CDA inspection confirming the built structure matches the approved drawings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Construction Questions About F-10

Can I add a second storey to my F-10 house?

Usually yes, after we check the existing foundation. Many F-10 houses were designed for a single storey, so the footings may need strengthening before additional load is applied. We inspect first, then design the extension and any strengthening work together and submit the revised drawings to CDA.

Is it worth renovating or should I rebuild?

Most F-10 houses have sound structures, which makes renovation the better value. We inspect the frame, footings and brickwork, and if they are in good condition we renovate. If we find serious structural problems, we say so and explain what a rebuild would involve — but we do not push a rebuild onto a house that does not need one.

How much does renovation cost in F-10?

Renovation cost depends almost entirely on how much you replace. Cosmetic work — paint, tiling, fittings — is a fraction of the cost of replacing wiring, plumbing and waterproofing throughout. We provide an itemised BOQ separating each element so you can decide how far to go and see exactly what each decision costs.
