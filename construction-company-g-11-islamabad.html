G-11 Construction & Renovation

G-11 Combines Good Access With a Still-Evolving Housing Stock

G-11 sits close to Kashmir Highway, which makes it one of the better-connected G-series sectors for moving materials and machinery onto site. Plot sizes run mostly to 10 Marla and 1 Kanal across the G-11/1 to G-11/4 sub-sectors, and the Markaz gives the sector a well-developed commercial core.

The housing stock is younger than G-6 or G-9, so the balance of work tilts toward new construction and first builds rather than heavy renovation — with a steady stream of second-storey additions on houses built a decade or two ago. That easier site access matters more than it sounds: it keeps concrete pours continuous and spoil removal quick, which protects both your timeline and your neighbours' patience.

Key point: On a house built 15 or 20 years ago, the original footings may or may not be sized for another storey. We verify before designing the addition, because strengthening later is far more disruptive than planning for it now.

Approvals & Compliance

CDA Approvals in G-11

New builds and storey additions follow the same approval route.

  • Building plan approval from CDA is required before mobilisation, on the basis of a complete architectural and structural drawing set.
  • Setback lines, covered-area ratio and permissible height depend on your plot size and street width — we confirm before designing.
  • Storey additions change the approved height and covered area, so a revised submission and approval are required.
  • Structural design must satisfy Islamabad's seismic zone requirements and is subject to structural vetting.
  • Completion certificates require CDA inspection confirming the built structure matches the approved drawings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Construction Questions About G-11

How much does construction cost in G-11?

It depends on plot size, how much you build over, and your specification. We quote from an itemised BOQ with named material brands rather than a blanket per-square-foot rate, so each stage is visible and you can decide where to specify premium and where standard specification is perfectly adequate.

Can you add a second floor to my G-11 house?

Usually yes, after we check the existing foundation. Houses built fifteen or twenty years ago were often designed for a single storey, so the footings may need strengthening before extra load is applied. We inspect first, then design the addition and any strengthening together and submit the revised drawings to CDA.

Do you handle CDA approval as well as the construction?

Yes. We prepare the architectural drawings, structural design and services layout, coordinate the CDA submission and build strictly to the approved set. Handling both means there is no gap between what was approved and what gets built — which is the usual reason completion certificates get delayed.
