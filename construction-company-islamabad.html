Structural Integrity

Our Engineering & Material Standards in Islamabad

Islamabad falls under Seismic Zone 2B (moderate to high earthquake risk). We adhere strictly to structural engineering best practices.

🏗️

Earthquake-Resistant RCC Framing

We use deformed De-formed Grade 60 steel (Mughal, Amreli, or Model Steel) with precise tie spacing, hook anchoring, and mechanical vibrators during every concrete pour.

🧱

First-Class Burnt Clay Bricks (Awwal)

Zero compromises on masonry. We hand-select high-density red clay bricks soaked in water tanks prior to laying to ensure maximum bond strength and thermal insulation.

💧

Multi-Layer Damp Proofing (DPC & Tanking)

Islamabad's winter rains and high water tables can cause wall efflorescence. We install multi-ply bitumen DPC barriers and basement tanking membranes.

📐

Laser-Levelled Plaster & Floors

Using digital levels and straight edges, our plasterers deliver seamless vertical walls ready for high-end paint, wallpapers, and precision porcelain tile fixing.

Questions About Hiring a Construction Company in Islamabad

Why should I choose Al Sadat Builders over generic labor contractors?

Generic labor contractors often lack civil engineering discipline, do not provide detailed BOQs, and cut corners on cement-to-sand ratios or steel ties. Al Sadat Builders offers 30+ years of audited reputation led by Syed Mujtaba Shah, binding contracts, milestone payments, and verified Grade 60 steel.

How long does it take to construct a 5 Marla or 10 Marla house in Islamabad?

For grey structure, typical construction takes 3.5 to 5 months depending on weather and curing schedules. For complete turnkey construction (including finishing, tiles, sanitary, paint, and woodwork), it takes 7 to 10 months. We establish a calendar milestone schedule prior to signing.

Can you manage construction for Overseas Pakistanis living in the UK, USA, or Gulf?

Yes! Over 40% of our clients reside abroad. We provide weekly high-resolution video progress calls, photographic material delivery proofs, transparent bank transfers tied to physical inspection milestones, and full digital record-keeping.
