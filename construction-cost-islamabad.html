Transparent Pricing Philosophy

How Much Does It Really Cost to Build a House in Islamabad?

Many online portals publish unrealistically low fixed "per square foot" rates simply to bait homeowners into signing contracts, only to hit them with massive price escalation claims halfway through the foundation or roof casting.

At Al Sadat Builders, we believe in radical transparency. Under CEO Syed Mujtaba Shah, we calculate every estimate using an Itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) based on your actual architectural drawings and prevailing wholesale market rates for steel, cement, and finishing materials.

Key Factors That Determine Your Final Cost:
  • Total Covered Area: Exact square footage of ground, first floor & mumty.
  • Basement Requirement: Deep excavation, retaining RCC walls & waterproofing add approx. 20-25% to structural costs.
  • Soil & Foundation Conditions: Zone 3 hillside plots or reclaimed soils require heavier raft or stepped foundations.
  • Finishing Grade: Standard, Executive, or Ultra-Luxury specifications for tiles, woodwork, and sanitary fittings.

Understanding BOQ

Why an Itemized BOQ Protects Homeowners from Scams

A **Bill of Quantities (BOQ)** is an itemized document listing every single construction task, the quantity of materials required (e.g. tons of steel, bags of cement, square feet of tiles), the unit price, and the total cost.

When you construct with Al Sadat Builders, you receive a detailed, binding BOQ before construction starts. You will know exactly:

  • Exact brand names committed in writing (e.g. Fauji OPC cement, Mughal 60-grade steel).
  • Exact milestone disbursement schedule linked to completed phases.
  • No unexpected claims or mid-project demands for additional cash.
  • Full audit rights on material receipts and on-site consignments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Construction Cost FAQs

Why do construction rates vary so widely between different contractors in Islamabad?

Cheap contractors often quote artificially low rates by using re-rolled sub-grade steel, commercial grade mortar mixes (1:8 instead of 1:6), unbranded non-porcelain tiles, and thin-gauge electrical cables. Al Sadat Builders never compromises on structural safety or electrical fire safety.

Does building a basement significantly increase construction cost?

Yes. A basement requires deep machine excavation, specialized soil retaining walls, heavy RCC slab casting, and multi-layer chemical tanking to prevent groundwater seepage. Typically, basement construction costs around 20% to 30% more per square foot than above-ground storeys, and it is quoted as a separate line item in our BOQ rather than blended into a flat rate.

Can we supply our own finishing materials while Al Sadat Builders does grey structure?

Absolutely! Many clients hire us for complete grey structure construction plus labor-rate finishing, allowing the homeowner to personally handpick imported tiles, sanitary fittings, and light fixtures. We provide full flexibility.
