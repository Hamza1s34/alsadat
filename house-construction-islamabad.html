Complete House Construction Scope of Work

A transparent breakdown of everything included in our turnkey home construction package.

🏗️

1. Structure & Civil Works

Excavation, termite proofing, anti-damp DPC barriers, Grade 60 steel frame, Fauji/Bestway cement, Margalla crush, and Awwal brickwork.

🚿

2. Concealed Plumbing & Sanitary

High-pressure PPRC water supply pipes, PVC drainage lines, underground & overhead water tanks, and branded sanitary fixtures (Porta / Grohe / Master).

⚡

3. Electrical & Smart Conduits

Pure copper wiring (Pakistan Cables / Fast Cables), Schneider / Clipsal switches, safety circuit breakers, and concealed AC and solar inverter conduit lines.

✨

4. Flooring, Marble & Tile Fixing

Porcelain glazed tiles for lounge, drawing room & bedrooms. Granite or imported marble on staircases, vanity counters, and kitchen platforms.

🚪

5. Joinery, Doors & Kitchen

Solid wood main entrance doors, semi-solid internal doors with high-security locks, and custom UV/acrylic sheet kitchen cabinetry with quartz counters.

🎨

6. Ceiling, Paint & Finishing

Gypsum plaster false ceiling designs, interior emulsion paints (Berger / Nippon / Dulux), exterior weather-shield rockwall plaster, and deep chemical site cleaning.
