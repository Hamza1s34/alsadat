Renovation Capabilities

Our Specialized Renovation & Repair Trades

From single bathroom upgrades to whole-house structural overhauls, we execute every trade with surgical precision.

🚿

Luxury Bathroom Renovation

Complete gut-rehab of outdated bathrooms. We dismantle old tiles, replace leaky concealed pipes, apply chemical waterproofing, and install imported floating vanities, walk-in glass shower enclosures, and designer wall tiles.

  • Old tile demolition & proper waste disposal
  • New high-pressure PPRC piping & drain lines
  • Multi-layer chemical waterproofing membrane
  • Concealed commodes, mixers & rain showers
🍳

Modern Kitchen Remodeling

Transform congested kitchens into spacious, open-concept culinary spaces with sleek acrylic/UV cabinets, quartz or granite breakfast counters, and optimized electrical points for modern appliances.

  • Open-plan island kitchen conversions
  • High-gloss UV & soft-close acrylic cabinetry
  • Heavy-duty granite & quartz counter installation
  • Concealed hood ventilation & LED strip conduits
✨

Marble & Porcelain Tile Fixing

Laser-straight tile installation by veteran craftsmen. Whether fixing 60x120cm porcelain floor tiles, Badal/Sunny Grey marble stairs, or granite vanity tops, we ensure 100% bond without hollow sounds.

  • Large format porcelain floor & wall tiling
  • Polymer-modified bond adhesives for zero pop-outs
  • Marble grinding, chemical crystallization & mirror polishing
  • Anti-fungal waterproof tile joint grouting
🛡️

Plaster Repair & Roof Waterproofing

Permanent solutions for efflorescence (shora), peeling paint, and roof leaks. We chisel away salt-damaged plaster, treat walls with chemical sealers, and re-plaster with enriched cement mortars.

  • Internal sponge plaster & exterior weather coat
  • Bitumen membrane & acrylic roof waterproofing
  • Basement damp proofing & chemical injection
  • Expansion joint sealing & parapet crack repair
🏠

Floor Additions & Modern Elevations

Adding a new floor or modernizing your facade? We reinforce existing foundations, construct lightweight brick/block masonry, and install contemporary louvers, rockwall textures, and glass balconies.

  • Second storey and rooftop servant quarter construction
  • Modern Spanish / Contemporary facade conversion
  • Boundary wall height extension & steel gate framing
  • Car porch expansion & terrace waterproofing
🔨

General Repairs & Partial Demolition

Safe knocking down of non-load-bearing partition walls to create spacious living areas, lintel beam installation, door frame widening, and structural foundation strengthening.

  • Controlled wall demolition & steel lintel supports
  • Chamber and sewer connection repairs
  • Window frame replacement with aluminum/UPVC
  • Crack stitching and epoxy mortar injection
How It Works

Our Hassle-Free Renovation Workflow

We take the stress out of home renovation with clear timelines and fixed itemized quotes.

1

Site Diagnostic Inspection

We visit your property to assess structural health, check dampness levels, and discuss your design aspirations.

2

Fixed-Price Itemized Quote

Receive a clear scope of work with material options (budget, executive, or luxury) and realistic timelines.

3

Dust Protection & Demolition

We seal off non-work areas with protective plastic sheeting and systematically remove old fittings and debris.

4

Master Craft Execution

Skilled master masons, plumbers, and tile fixers carry out installations under active supervisor checks.

5

Deep Clean & Handover

We conduct pressure tests on all plumbing, inspect tile alignment, perform chemical cleaning, and hand over your upgraded home.
