How We Work

Our Transparent 5-Step Construction Process

No guesswork, no stress. We guide you through an orderly, professional journey from empty plot to key handover.

1

Consultation & Site Visit

We review your plot coordinates, soil type, society bylaws, and architectural desires at no initial cost.

2

Detailed BOQ & Quotation

3

Legal Contract & Schedule
