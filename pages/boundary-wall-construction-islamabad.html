Boundary Wall Construction in Islamabad & Bani Gala

Reinforced Brick & Block Walls, Hillside Retaining Structures, Foundation Trenches & Plaster Coping

Perimeter Security & Defense

Heavy-Duty Boundary Walls Built to Withstand Islamabad’s Soil & Hillside Pressures

Whether securing an open plot against encroachers in **Bani Gala, Park View City, or Gulberg**, or replacing a cracked and leaning perimeter wall around an existing house, a boundary wall must be engineered with proper foundation depth and reinforced vertical pillars.

In hilly terrain such as **Bani Gala**, rainfall runoff and hillside earth pressure cause poorly constructed 4.5-inch single-brick walls to tilt or collapse. At **Al Sadat Builders**, CEO **Syed Mujtaba Shah** ensures:

  • Minimum 2.5 to 3.5-foot deep foundation footings with lean concrete base
  • Grade 60 steel RCC vertical tie pillars every 10 to 12 feet
  • 9-inch solid brick or heavy hollow concrete block masonry
  • Weep holes for water drainage on hillside retaining boundary walls
  • Weather-proof cement plaster and angled concrete coping on top
