Bahria Design Wing Experience

Bahria Enclave's Hills Are Beautiful — and They Change How You Build

Bahria Town Rawalpindi and Bahria Enclave share a developer, but they are very different construction environments. Bahria Town's developed phases are largely flat and serviced, while Bahria Enclave is built into genuinely steep terrain where many plots required cut-and-fill before they could be handed over — and where the exposed cut faces and slope stability become your problem the moment you start excavating.

On top of the terrain sits a formal approval process through the Bahria Design Wing, which reviews the architectural design and elevation before construction begins, plus mandatory rainwater harvesting and documented security clearance for site labour. Al Sadat Builders works through that process regularly: we prepare the design package for review, size the retaining and drainage works to the actual plot, and build to the approved design.

Key point: In Bahria Enclave we treat slope stability and drainage as structural work, not landscaping. Retaining walls, toe drains and cut-face protection go into the BOQ from day one.

Approvals & Compliance

Bahria Approvals & Compliance

Bahria enforces its own design and construction rules independently of CDA and RDA.

  • Architectural design and elevation must be submitted to the Bahria Design Wing and approved before construction starts.
  • Rainwater harvesting is a construction requirement in Bahria, not an optional upgrade — the system must be built into the plot.
  • Site labour requires documented security clearance, and material movement follows the society's gate and timing procedures.
  • Bahria Enclave plots on steep or cut terrain need slope stability and drainage works designed alongside the structure.
  • Bahria Town Rawalpindi falls under RDA's wider jurisdiction, so certain submissions and completion requirements route through RDA — we confirm which applies to your plot.

Frequently Asked Questions

Construction Questions About Bahria Town & Bahria Enclave

Do you handle Bahria Design Wing approval?

Yes. We prepare the architectural design, elevation package and supporting drawings to Bahria's submission format, then coordinate the Design Wing review. If you already have a design you like, we work from it and prepare the structural and submission documentation around it.

Is Bahria Enclave harder to build in than Bahria Town?

The approvals process is similar, but the terrain is very different. Bahria Enclave's hills mean many plots sit on graded or cut ground, so retaining walls, toe drains and slope protection become part of the structural scope. On flat Bahria Town phases, that work is usually minimal. This is why an Enclave BOQ will always carry slope-related line items that a flat-phase BOQ will not.

How much does construction cost in Bahria Town or Enclave?

Flat Bahria Town phases price close to standard Islamabad rates. Bahria Enclave generally prices higher because of slope work, retaining structures and material handling on graded roads. We quote from an itemised BOQ after a free on-site survey, with terrain work shown as separate line items.

Can you handle rainwater harvesting compliance?

Yes. Rainwater harvesting is a construction requirement in Bahria, and we design and build the collection and recharge system into the plot during construction. Trying to add it after the house is finished usually means breaking finished surfaces, so it belongs in the original scope.
