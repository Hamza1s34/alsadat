DHA Building Control Experience

DHA Rewards Discipline — and Punishes Anyone Who Skips the Approvals

DHA Islamabad runs on its own Building Control Directorate, and its rules are enforced. Setback lines, covered-area ratios, height limits, basement depth, sewer connection procedure and the elevation design itself all have to be submitted and approved before a single shovel goes in. Work that starts without approval gets stopped — and rectifying it afterwards costs far more than doing it right the first time.

Al Sadat Builders has built across DHA Phases 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6, and we prepare the submission set ourselves: architectural drawings, structural design, services layout and the elevation package. Because we handle both the paperwork and the execution, there is no gap between what was approved and what actually gets built on your plot.

Key point: We submit your drawings to DHA Building Control before mobilisation, and we build strictly to the approved set — so your completion certificate is not held up by unapproved deviations.

Local Conditions

What Makes a DHA Build Different

DHA is a planned society, but the execution rules are stricter and the inspection gates are real.

🏛️

DHA Building Control Submission

Architectural drawings, structural design, elevation package and services layout all go in as a set. We prepare and coordinate the submission rather than leaving you to run between consultants.

📐

Setbacks, Coverage Ratio & Height Limits

DHA enforces its own front, rear and side setback lines plus maximum covered area and height. We test the layout against those limits before finalising, so the design is approvable from the start.

🏗️

Basement Excavation & Dewatering

Basements are common on larger DHA plots. They need shoring design, a dewatering plan for the water table, and full tanking — not just a bitumen coat on the outside face.

🔐

Labour Security Clearance & Site Discipline

DHA requires cleared, documented labour and controlled site access. We maintain worker records, keep the site fenced and manage material movement so neighbours and society rules are respected.

Approvals & Compliance

DHA Approvals & Compliance

The sequence matters as much as the drawings.

  • Building plan approval from the DHA Building Control Directorate is required before mobilisation, based on a complete drawing set including structural design.
  • Setback lines, maximum covered area and height limits are set by DHA and vary by phase and plot size — we verify your specific plot before designing.
  • Basements require a shoring and dewatering approach as part of the submission, not as an afterthought once excavation starts.
  • Sewer and drainage connections must follow DHA's specified procedure, including inspection before backfilling.
  • A completion certificate is issued only after DHA inspection confirms the built structure matches the approved drawings — which is why we build to the approved set without unapproved deviations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Construction Questions About DHA Islamabad

Do you handle DHA Building Control approval, or do we need our own architect?

We handle it. Al Sadat Builders prepares the architectural drawings, structural design, elevation package and services layout, then coordinates the submission to the DHA Building Control Directorate. If you already have an architect's design, we work from those drawings and prepare the structural and submission package around them.

Can you build a basement in DHA Islamabad?

Yes. Basements are common on larger DHA plots and we build them regularly. The critical parts are shoring design for the excavation, a dewatering plan for the water table, and proper full tanking on the external faces. A thin bitumen coat on a basement wall is not waterproofing — moisture will find its way through and you will be dealing with damp and efflorescence for the life of the house.

How much does construction cost in DHA Islamabad?

DHA sits at the upper end of Islamabad rates because of stricter finishing expectations, deeper excavation where basements are involved, and premium material specifications. We quote from an itemised BOQ with named material brands, not a blanket per-square-foot number, so you can see exactly where the money is going and adjust specifications deliberately.

Do you build in all DHA Islamabad phases?

Yes — we work across Phases 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6. Each phase has its own bylaw nuances and inspection process, so tell us your phase and plot number when you enquire and we will confirm the specific requirements that apply.
