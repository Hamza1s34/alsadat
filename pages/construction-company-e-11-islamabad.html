E-11 New Builds & Grey Structure

E-11 Is the Newest Major Sector — and Mostly a First-Build Market

E-11 is among the last of Islamabad's major CDA sectors to be built out, and its plot structure reflects that. Alongside the 1 Kanal and 10 Marla plots found elsewhere in the city, E-11 has a substantial number of smaller 5 and 8 Marla plots — which makes it accessible to first-time builders in a way that F-6 or F-7 simply is not. The sub-sectors run E-11/1 through E-11/4, served by the E-11 Markaz.

Because so much of the sector was built recently, most E-11 projects are first builds rather than renovations. That means clean ground, no existing structure to work around, and a real opportunity to get the foundation, waterproofing and services right from the beginning — which is precisely where we concentrate effort, because those are the elements you cannot easily change later.

Key point: On a first build you get exactly one chance to get the foundation and waterproofing right. Everything above them can be upgraded later; they cannot.

Local Conditions

What Matters on an E-11 Build

First builds concentrate all the important decisions at the beginning.

🏗️

Getting the Foundation Right First Time

On a new plot there is no existing structure to reveal problems later. Excavation depth, footing size, anti-termite treatment and DPC are all one-shot decisions that determine the life of the building.

📐

Small Plots, Efficient Plans

E-11's 5 and 8 Marla plots demand careful planning. Stair position, light wells and storage placement decide whether a compact house feels comfortable or cramped.

🚰

Water Storage & Bore Provision

Sector water supply is not always constant. We size underground and overhead storage generously, and plan bore provision where the plot and sub-sector make it feasible.

🛣️

Access on Newly Built Streets

On streets where building is still active, access and staging need coordination with neighbours. We sequence deliveries and spoil removal so the street stays usable for everyone.

Approvals & Compliance

CDA Approvals in E-11

Approval is required before mobilisation on every plot in the sector.

  • Building plan approval from CDA Building Control is required before construction begins on any plot.
  • Setback lines, covered-area ratio and maximum height depend on your plot size and street width — we verify your specific plot before designing.
  • Structural design must satisfy Islamabad's seismic zone requirements and undergoes structural vetting.
  • Service connections for water, sewer and drainage follow CDA's specified procedure for the sub-sector.
  • A completion certificate is issued after CDA inspection confirms the built structure matches the approved drawings without deviations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Construction Questions About E-11

How much does it cost to build a 5 Marla house in E-11?

It depends on specification and how much of the plot you build over. We provide an itemised BOQ with named material brands after a free site visit — grey structure and finishing shown separately — so you can see the cost of each stage and make deliberate choices rather than accepting a single bundled number.

Is E-11 a good sector for a first home build?

It is one of the more accessible CDA sectors for first-time builders because of the smaller 5 and 8 Marla plot sizes and the fact that much of the sector is still being built out. The trade-off is that infrastructure and neighbouring construction are still active in places, which we plan around in the project sequence.

Do you handle CDA approval for a new build in E-11?

Yes. We prepare the architectural drawings, structural design and services layout, coordinate the CDA submission and build strictly to the approved set. Building without approval risks a stop-work notice and delays the completion certificate — on a first build that is a delay you do not want.

Can you build grey structure only, or do you only do turnkey?

Both. Many E-11 clients build the grey structure first and complete finishing in stages as budget allows. We hand over a clean, documented shell built to full specification so that whenever you are ready to finish, the starting point is known and structurally sound.
