F-11 New Builds & Renovation

F-11 Has More Empty and Newly Built Plots Than Its Neighbours

F-11 was developed later than F-6, F-7 and F-10, and its build-out is still visibly uneven — fully established streets sit a short walk from plots that were only recently built or are still empty. Plot sizes run mostly to 10 Marla and 1 Kanal across the F-11/1 to F-11/4 sub-sectors, with the F-11 Markaz serving as the commercial centre.

That mix means F-11 projects are more often first builds and grey-structure jobs than the deep renovation work you find in the older sectors. Building on a plot with empty or partially built neighbours brings its own practical set of issues — site access, water availability during construction, and protecting your finished work from adjacent excavation. Al Sadat Builders plans the sequence around those conditions.

Key point: On a plot with construction happening next door, your newly completed finishes are at risk from their excavation. We plan party-wall protection and external waterproofing as part of your scope, not as an afterthought.

Local Conditions

What Matters on an F-11 Project

A sector mid build-out creates conditions you do not get in a fully settled one.

🚧

Adjacent Construction & Party-Wall Protection

Excavation next door can undermine your foundation and expose your walls. We design footings and external waterproofing with neighbouring construction in mind and protect shared boundaries.

🚰

Water for Construction & Long-Term Storage

Building water is not always freely available on a developing street. We plan construction water supply and, separately, size your permanent underground and overhead tanks so the household is not affected by supply variability.

📐

First-Build Design Within CDA Limits

On a fresh plot there is no existing footprint to work around, but the setback, coverage and height rules still define what you can build. We design to those limits from the first sketch.

🧱

Grey Structure Quality on New Builds

A large share of F-11 work is grey structure. Steel grade, tie spacing, concrete mix and curing are what determine whether the house is still sound in thirty years, and that is where we refuse to economise.

Approvals & Compliance

CDA Approvals in F-11

First builds on a developing street still follow the standard CDA route.

  • Building plan approval from CDA Building Control is required before construction begins, based on the full drawing set.
  • Setback lines, covered-area ratio and maximum permissible height depend on your plot size and street width — we verify before designing.
  • Structural design must satisfy Islamabad's seismic zone requirements and undergoes structural vetting.
  • Party-wall and boundary treatment is governed by CDA rules, which matters where neighbouring plots are under construction.
  • A completion certificate is issued after CDA inspection confirms the structure matches the approved drawings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Construction Questions About F-11

How much does it cost to build a 10 Marla house in F-11?

It depends on specification and how much of the plot you build over. We provide an itemised BOQ with named material brands after a free site visit, with grey structure and finishing costed as separate sections. That structure lets you see exactly where the money goes and make deliberate choices about where to upgrade.

Do you build grey structure only in F-11?

Yes, and it is a common request here. We build the complete grey structure to specification — foundation, RCC frame, brickwork, conduits and rough plumbing — and hand over a clean, documented shell. You can then finish it in stages or with a different contractor, starting from a known sound state.

My neighbour is also building. Does that affect my project?

It can, and it is worth planning for. Adjacent excavation can undermine a boundary, and water from their site can damage your new finishes. We protect shared boundaries, waterproof external faces on the affected side, and where necessary discuss sequencing so the two projects do not damage each other.
