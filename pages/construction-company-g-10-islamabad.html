G-10 Construction & Renovation

G-10 Is a Straightforward Family Sector — and That Suits Careful Building

G-10 is a settled residential sector built around its own Markaz, with plot sizes running mostly to 10 Marla and 1 Kanal across the G-10/1 to G-10/4 sub-sectors. Unlike the commercial-heavy sectors nearby, it is predominantly residential, which makes for cleaner working conditions and a more consistent standard along each street.

Housing stock in G-10 is a genuine mix: original builds that have never been touched, houses renovated once a decade ago, and a steady trickle of demolish-and-rebuild projects where the land value now justifies a new house. Because the sector is residential and reasonably accessible, G-10 is a good environment for careful, unhurried construction — and we build these projects with the same structural standard we apply to the highest-specification work elsewhere.

Key point: A 10 Marla family home in G-10 gets the same Grade 60 steel, the same cure schedule and the same waterproofing detail as a 2 Kanal villa. Specification changes; structural standard does not.

What We Handle in G-10

Practical issues that shape how a G-10 build runs.

Making a 10 Marla Plot Work Hard

On a fixed footprint, stair position, light wells and circulation decide whether the house feels cramped or comfortable. We plan those first rather than squeezing rooms into whatever is left.

💧

Waterproofing the Whole Envelope

Roof, bathrooms, plinth and external faces all need proper treatment in an Islamabad climate. We build the waterproofing into the structural stage rather than applying it as a final decoration.

⬆️

Storey Additions & Existing Footings

Many G-10 houses were built as single storeys. Before adding above, we verify the existing foundation and design strengthening where it is needed.

🔌

Upgrading Services in Dated Houses

Where a house is being renovated rather than rebuilt, replacing wiring and plumbing at that point is far cheaper than reopening finished walls two years later.

Approvals & Compliance

CDA Approvals in G-10

Straightforward sector, but the drawing set still has to be complete.

  • Building plan approval from CDA Building Control is required before construction, on the basis of a complete drawing set.
  • Setback lines, covered-area ratio and height limits depend on plot size and street width — we verify your plot before design.
  • Structural design must meet Islamabad's seismic zone requirements and undergoes structural vetting.
  • Extensions and storey additions require a revised submission and approval, not just new construction.
  • A completion certificate is issued after CDA inspection confirms the structure matches the approved drawings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Construction Questions About G-10

What does it cost to build a 10 Marla house in G-10?

The cost depends on how much of the plot you build over and the specification you choose. We do not quote a flat per-square-foot figure — after a free site visit you get an itemised BOQ with named material brands, with grey structure and finishing shown as separate sections so you can see the cost of each stage and adjust deliberately.

Do you build grey structure only in G-10?

Yes. Lots of clients build the grey structure first and complete the finishing later in stages as funds allow. We hand over a clean, documented shell built to full specification, so whenever you are ready to finish, the starting point is known and sound.

Can I extend my existing G-10 house instead of rebuilding?

In most cases yes. We inspect the existing structure first — if the footings and walls are sound, an extension or second storey is usually the better value. If we find structural problems, we will tell you honestly and explain what a rebuild would cost, so the decision is based on the actual condition of your house rather than an assumption.
