G-6 Renovation & Rebuild

G-6 Is Old, Central and Constantly Under Pressure

G-6 is one of Islamabad's oldest sectors, and it sits immediately against the Blue Area and the Melody Market commercial centre. The result is a sector with a genuinely mixed character: established family homes on quiet interior streets, commercial frontage along the main roads, and decades of incremental modification layered onto the original housing stock.

That layering is what makes G-6 work different. Structures here have often been altered several times — walls opened, rooms added, services extended — usually without any consistent structural record. Before we quote anything in G-6 we survey what is actually there, because the original drawings rarely describe the house that is standing on the plot today.

Key point: In G-6, unrecorded past modifications are the biggest hidden risk. We survey the structure as-built rather than trusting drawings that may describe a very different house.

Local Conditions

What Makes G-6 Projects Tricky

Age, density and mixed-use pressure all compound each other here.

🔍

Surveying Decades of Unrecorded Modifications

Walls opened, extensions added, services rerouted — often without documentation. We inspect the structure as it actually stands before recommending any scope of work.

🧱

Load-Bearing Walls & Limited Openings

Many G-6 houses are built on load-bearing brick. Opening a plan requires properly designed beams and lintels, and some walls genuinely cannot be removed without compromising the structure.

🚗

Tight Streets & Parking Pressure

G-6's interior streets are narrow and heavily parked, with commercial traffic on the main roads. Excavation spoil, concrete deliveries and material staging need tight scheduling to stay workable.

🏬

Residential Work Beside Commercial Use

Where homes sit alongside commercial frontage, working hours, noise and site security need managing differently than on a purely residential street.

Approvals & Compliance

CDA Approvals in G-6

Decades of unauthorised alterations are common here — approvals catch them.

  • Building plan approval from CDA is required for rebuilds, extensions and structural alterations.
  • Setback lines and covered-area limits may differ from those the original house was built under — we confirm the current rules for your plot.
  • Structural design must meet Islamabad's seismic zone requirements and is subject to CDA structural vetting.
  • Where a property has mixed residential and commercial use, additional CDA conditions may apply — we confirm these before design.
  • Completion certificates require CDA inspection confirming the built structure matches the approved drawings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Construction Questions About G-6

My G-6 house has been modified over the years. Can you still work with it?

Yes, and this is normal in G-6. The key step is surveying the structure as it actually stands rather than relying on original drawings that may no longer describe the house. We inspect the footings, walls and any past structural alterations, then tell you clearly what can be kept, what needs strengthening and what should be replaced.

Can I remove an interior wall to open up my G-6 house?

Sometimes. In a load-bearing brick house, an interior wall may be carrying part of the floor or roof load — removing it without a properly designed beam above creates a real structural problem. We assess which walls are load-bearing and, where removal is possible, design the supporting beam and lintel detail to make it safe.

Do you handle CDA approval for renovation work in G-6?

Yes. Structural alterations and extensions require CDA approval, not just new builds. We prepare the drawing set, coordinate the submission and build to the approved design so your completion certificate is not held up later by unapproved changes.
