Local Conditions

What Matters on a G-9 Project

Density and age define the working conditions here.

💧

Rising Damp, Efflorescence & Waterproofing

Older G-9 houses commonly show salt staining and damp patches. We trace it to its source — failing DPC, absent plinth protection, poor drainage falls — and correct the cause before new finishes are applied.

🔌

Aging Electrical & Plumbing Services

Original wiring and pipework rarely suit modern loads. We replace services properly during renovation rather than adding to a system already past its design life.

🚧

Narrow Interior Streets & Access

G-9's internal streets are narrow and busy. Excavation spoil removal, concrete delivery and material staging need careful scheduling to keep the street usable for neighbours.

⬆️

Storey Additions Within Height Limits

Adding an upper floor requires both structural verification of the existing footings and compliance with CDA's height and covered-area limits — we check both before designing.

Approvals & Compliance

CDA Approvals in G-9

Adding a floor is the most common submission we handle in this sector.

  • Building plan approval from CDA is required for new construction, extensions and structural alterations.
  • Height and covered-area limits apply per plot and street — adding a storey requires a revised submission and approval.
  • Setback lines are set by CDA and we verify the current requirements for your specific plot before designing.
  • Structural design must satisfy Islamabad's seismic zone requirements and is subject to structural vetting.
  • Completion certificates follow CDA inspection confirming the built structure matches the approved drawings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Construction Questions About G-9

Why do the walls in my older G-9 house keep getting damp patches?

In almost every case it is moisture from below or outside the wall rather than a paint failure. Failing damp-proof course, missing plinth protection, poor drainage falls around the building and unsealed external faces all let water into the masonry, and the salts it carries show up as efflorescence. Repainting over it looks fine for one season and then returns. We trace and correct the source, then finish the wall properly.

Can I add another floor to my G-9 house?

Often yes, subject to two checks. First, the existing footings and walls have to be capable of carrying the extra load — many single-storey houses in this sector were not designed for a second floor and need strengthening. Second, the addition has to comply with CDA's height and covered-area limits, which means a revised submission and approval before work starts.

How much does renovation cost in G-9?

It depends on how much you replace. A targeted kitchen and bathroom renovation costs a fraction of a whole-house modernisation with full service replacement. We provide an itemised BOQ separating structure, waterproofing, services and finishing so you can see the cost of each element and decide the scope deliberately.
