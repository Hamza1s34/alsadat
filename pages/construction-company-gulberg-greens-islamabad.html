Farmhouse & Large-Plot Builders

Big Plots Ask Different Questions

Gulberg Greens was planned as a farmhouse belt, and the plots show it. Instead of the 5 and 10 Marla footprints that dominate the rest of Islamabad, you are working with 1, 2, 4 Kanal and larger parcels — often with wide frontages and a lot of open ground around the building itself.

That scale changes the engineering brief. Long unsupported spans need proper column and beam layouts rather than guesswork. A farmhouse also needs its own water supply strategy, its own sewerage or septic arrangement, a perimeter boundary wall with properly founded piers, and drainage across open ground that has nothing to do with a municipal system. Al Sadat Builders builds this scope as one coordinated package — structure, boundary, services and finishing — rather than leaving you to stitch together four different contractors.

Key point: On farmhouse plots, the perimeter boundary wall and gate structure are a structural build in their own right. Under-sizing the footings is one of the most common and most expensive mistakes on large plots.

Local Conditions

What We Handle Differently on Farmhouse Plots

Large plots introduce scope that simply does not exist on a 5 Marla city plot.

📏

Long Spans & Wide Frontages

Wide farmhouse layouts invite long unsupported spans. We design the column and beam grid properly so the structure carries the load without excessive deflection or cracking.

🧱

Perimeter Boundary Walls & Gate Piers

A farmhouse perimeter is a long run of wall needing founded piers at correct intervals. We size footings to the wall height and soil, and build the gate structure as an engineered element.

💧

Independent Water & Sewerage Strategy

Farmhouse plots are usually outside municipal services. We plan bore water, underground and overhead storage, septic tanks and soakage pits as a complete system sized to the house.

🌧️

Open-Ground Drainage & Ground Levels

With nothing built around you, rainwater has to be directed deliberately. We set site levels and drainage falls so water moves away from the building and does not pond against the boundary.

Approvals & Compliance

Approvals & Compliance in Gulberg

Farmhouse zones have their own land-use and setback logic.

  • Gulberg operates under its own society administration with rules on plot coverage, building height and setback from the plot boundary — we confirm the specific requirements for your block.
  • Farmhouse plots carry land-use conditions that limit how much of the plot can be built over, so the layout must be planned against the permitted coverage.
  • Perimeter boundary wall heights and gate positions are subject to society rules, and we confirm these before construction rather than after.
  • Service connections and sewerage arrangements follow the society's procedure for the specific block or phase.
  • We prepare the drawing set and coordinate submission so the built structure matches what was approved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Construction Questions About Gulberg Greens & Residencia

Do you build farmhouses in Gulberg Greens?

Yes. Farmhouse construction is a core part of our work in Gulberg Greens — single and double storey residences on 1, 2 and 4 Kanal plots, including the perimeter boundary wall, gate structure and full external services as one coordinated package.

Can you build the perimeter boundary wall as well as the house?

Yes, and we recommend it as a single scope. The boundary wall, gate piers and the main building should share the same foundation design logic and the same site level plan. Splitting them between contractors is how you end up with a wall that settles away from the gate pier, or drainage that ponds against one side of the boundary.

How is farmhouse construction priced?

Farmhouse pricing depends far more on scope than on plot size. A 2 Kanal plot with a modest single-storey house costs a fraction of the same plot built out with a double-storey residence and outbuildings. We quote from an itemised BOQ covering structure, boundary, services and finishing as separate sections so you can add or drop scope deliberately.

How long does a farmhouse take to build?

Large plots take longer than city plots mainly because of the external scope — boundary walls, long drainage runs and independent service systems. Grey structure and turnkey timelines both extend with plot size and built-up area. We set a milestone calendar before mobilisation.
