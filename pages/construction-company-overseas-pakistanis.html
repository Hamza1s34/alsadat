Remote Build Management

The Real Risk Is Not Distance — It Is Not Knowing What Is Happening

Every overseas client we work with is worried about the same thing: sending money to Pakistan and having no idea what it is actually being spent on. That is a reasonable fear. A build that is not being watched can quietly drift on quantity of steel, cement brand, slab thickness and waterproofing — and you will only find out when you visit years later and the cracks appear.

Al Sadat Builders manages overseas projects around a simple principle: you should never have to ask what happened this week. You get a fixed reporting schedule, a video walkthrough on a set day, photographs dated and tied to physical progress, and material delivery records you can match against your own ledger. Payments release against completed, inspected milestones rather than a monthly demand.

Key point: Payments are linked to milestones that have already been completed and recorded on video — not to a schedule. If a milestone has not been reached, it is not billed.

How We Remove the Distance Problem

Four concrete mechanisms that replace being physically present on site.

📹

Scheduled Weekly Video Calls

A fixed day and time each week — 30 to 45 minutes, walking the site with you. You see the slab, the steel spacing, the plaster, the finishing. No waiting for you to chase an update.

Dated Material Delivery Records

Every cement, steel and aggregate delivery is photographed with the delivery challan, and the records are shared with you. You can reconcile them against what you have paid for.

Milestone-Linked Bank Payments

Funds move through formal banking against completed milestones, not cash demands. Each release has a documented basis: what was finished, what was verified, what comes next.

You sign off an itemised BOQ before mobilisation. Any genuine variation is documented and priced before the work is done, so the final account does not surprise you.

Project Types

What Overseas Clients Usually Build

Most overseas projects fall into one of these four patterns.

Family Home for Return

A full turnkey house you intend to live in when you move back, built to a specification you would choose for your own permanent home — often 10 Marla or 1 Kanal.

Investment Build

A practical, well-built house designed for rental yield rather than personal taste, with specification choices driven by what the local rental market actually values.

Grey Structure First

Many overseas owners build the structure while funds are available and complete finishing later, spreading the cost across visits or across a longer savings period.

Finishing & Repair Only

A house built earlier by someone else, now needing proper plaster, waterproofing, tile work and finishing brought up to standard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Construction Questions About Overseas Pakistanis

How do I monitor construction if I live in the UK, USA, Canada or the Gulf?

You get a scheduled weekly video walkthrough on a fixed day and time, chosen to suit your time zone. We walk the site with you, show you the work in progress, and answer questions live. Between calls, dated photographs of material deliveries and completed stages are shared with you so there is a continuous record rather than a weekly snapshot.

How do payments work for overseas clients?

Payments are milestone-linked rather than calendar-based. Before mobilisation you approve an itemised BOQ with the milestone schedule. When a milestone is completed and verified on video, the corresponding payment is released through formal banking. If a milestone has not been reached, it is not billed.

Can you take over a half-finished house that another contractor abandoned?

Yes, and this is a common request. We survey the existing structure first — checking footings, column alignment, steel quality and concrete condition where it can be assessed — and give you an honest written assessment of what can be kept and what needs remediation before we quote for completing it.

Do you work with my family member or attorney in Pakistan?

Yes. Many overseas clients have a relative or a local attorney handling day-to-day matters. We work with them directly, copy you on instructions, and document decisions in writing so there is a single record that everyone can see. We do not take verbal instructions from a third party that contradict what you have approved.

How much should I budget for construction in Islamabad?

It depends on plot size and specification level, and rates move with steel and cement prices. We do not publish a single number that will be wrong by the time you read it. Send us your plot details and we will give you an itemised BOQ with current material rates and named brands, which you can compare directly against other quotes.
