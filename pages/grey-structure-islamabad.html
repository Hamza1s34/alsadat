Precision Civil Engineering, Certified Grade 60 Rebar & High-Strength Concrete Slabs

The Foundation of Your Home

Why Grey Structure Quality Defines Your Home’s Lifespan

While cosmetic finishes like tiles and paints can always be replaced years down the road, your grey structure can never be redone. A weak foundation, sub-standard cement mix, or rusted low-grade steel will lead to structural cracks, slab sags, and costly damp seepage forever.

At Al Sadat Builders, grey structure construction is our foundational pride. Supervised personally on-site by Syed Mujtaba Shah (30+ years field experience), we follow strict civil engineering protocols for excavation depths, soil compaction, water curing (minimum 14 days for slabs), and shuttering alignment.

Certified Rebar Only: We exclusively procure Grade 60 deformed rebar from prime mills (Mughal / Amreli / Model Steel) accompanied by laboratory mill test certificates.

Comprehensive Scope

What Is Included in Our Grey Structure Contract?

No vague clauses. Here is the exact scope of work carried out from plot groundbreaking to plaster completion.

⛏️

1. Excavation & Anti-Termite Treatment

  • Machine excavation according to architectural soil bearing levels
  • Chemical anti-termite spray across plot base and foundation trenches
  • Compacted gravel/sand bed laying and roller compaction
  • Lean concrete (P.C.C 1:4:8) under all footings
🏗️

2. RCC Footings, Plinth & Beams

  • Raft or strip footings with Grade 60 steel cage reinforcement
  • Plinth beam casting with heavy seismic tie bars
  • Bitumen coating and damp proof course (DPC) membrane installation
  • Backfilling with selected pit sand/ghassu & flooded compaction
🧱

3. Brick Masonry & Block Work

  • First-class burnt red clay bricks (Awwal Bhatta Bricks)
  • 1:6 cement-sand mortar ratio with properly racked joints
  • 9-inch external load-bearing walls and 4.5-inch internal partitions
  • Water curing of bricks prior to and after masonry laying
🏛️

4. Columns, Lintel Beams & Slabs

  • Heavy RCC vertical columns tied to foundation footings
  • Steel shuttering with clean release oil for smooth finish
  • Roof slab casting with mechanical needle vibrator compaction
  • 14 to 21 days water ponding curing for crack prevention
🚿

5. Underground & Overhead Tanks

  • RCC underground water storage tank with waterproofing additive
  • Overhead RCC water tank with dual-coat water barrier
  • Rough plumbing supply conduits (PPRC) embedded in structure
  • Waste soil drainage lines (PVC) with proper gravity slope
⚡

6. Electrical Conduits & Boundary Wall

  • Concealed PVC electrical pipe routing in slabs and walls
  • Fan hook boxes and switchboard cavity boxes placed
  • Complete perimeter boundary wall with reinforced pillars
  • Internal and external cement plaster work (smooth sponge finish)
