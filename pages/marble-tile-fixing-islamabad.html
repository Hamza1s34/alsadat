Master Craftsmanship

Flawless Tile & Marble Installation That Elevates Your Home

Even the most expensive imported Italian tiles or Pakistani Badal marble will look substandard if installed by unskilled laborers using uneven mortar beds, crooked spacers, or hollow adhesive spots. Hollow tiles crack under furniture weight, retain water in bathrooms, and pop loose within a couple of years.

At Al Sadat Builders, tile and marble installation is supervised directly by Syed Mujtaba Shah, drawing on over 30 years of specialized masonry experience. We use digital laser levels, premium polymer-modified bond mortars (Bondex / Sika / Master), tile vibration machines, and anti-fungal epoxy grouts.
