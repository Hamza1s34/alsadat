Plaster Work & Wall Repair in Islamabad

Laser-Straight Internal Sponge Plaster, Weatherproof Exterior Coatings & Permanent Salt (Shora) Elimination

Flawless Wall Finish

Smooth, Crack-Free Plastering Backed by 30+ Years Experience

High-end interior paints, wall paneling, and wallpapers depend 100% on the smoothness and vertical accuracy of your plaster. Wavy plaster, improper cement-to-sand ratios, or inadequate water curing cause spiderweb hairline cracks and hollow detachment within months.

At Al Sadat Builders, CEO Syed Mujtaba Shah directs specialized plastering crews equipped with 10-foot aluminum straightedges, plumb bobs, corner beading, and anti-shrinkage polymers. Whether completing the internal walls of a new villa in **Bani Gala** or restoring weather-damaged walls in **F-10**, we deliver walls that look like glass.

Permanent Salt Efflorescence (Shora) Treatment: If dampness has damaged your walls, simple repainting fails within weeks. We chisel away the infected plaster, neutralize chemical salts with deep-penetrating acid wash, apply water-barrier polymer slurry, and re-plaster with enriched sand mortar.
